ℹ️ Important Guidelines for Blood Request

Blood requests are handled with utmost priority and responsibility. Please ensure that all information provided is accurate and genuine. This helps hospitals and donors respond quickly and effectively.

  • 🩸 Blood Group Accuracy: Always confirm the required blood group with the hospital before submitting.
  • 📦 Units Required: Request only the necessary number of units to avoid shortage for others.
  • 🚑 Emergency Requests: Emergency cases are instantly notified to available donors and hospitals.
  • ⏱️ Normal Requests: Normal requests may take additional time based on availability.
  • 🏥 Hospital Verification: The hospital may contact you to verify patient details before approval.
  • 📞 Contact Responsibility: Ensure your contact details are correct and reachable at all times.
  • 🔒 Privacy Protection: Your personal information is securely stored and never shared publicly.
  • 🤝 Donor Availability: Blood availability depends on donor response and current stock.

❤️ LifeFlow Mission:
Our mission is to connect patients, donors, and hospitals efficiently to save lives during critical situations.

⚠️ Submitting false, misleading, or unnecessary blood requests may result in temporary or permanent account restrictions.
